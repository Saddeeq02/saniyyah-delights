The Craft & Heritage

Where Kano’s Sweet Traditions Meet Modern Elegance

In Northern Nigeria, treats like Gullisuwa and Alewar Madara are more than mere sweets — they are symbols of hospitality, celebration, and joy.

Founded by Saniyyah Abdulkadir Ismail, Saniyyah’s Delights was born out of a profound passion for preserving these authentic flavors while elevating them with gourmet presentation, pure premium milk powder, and uncompromising hygiene.

✨

Made Fresh Daily

No stale stockpiles. Every batch of Gullisuwa and Gireba is prepared fresh to order.

🍯

Finest Ingredients

Pure whole-cream powdered milk, natural sweeteners, and rich aromatic cardamoms.

📦

Sealed & Beautiful Packaging

Air-tight, elegant gift boxes and tubs that preserve maximum crispness and crunch.
